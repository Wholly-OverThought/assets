# Life Improvement Course / Subscription — Idea Log

A place to catch ideas for the bigger pivot, separate from the individual product notes (`time-management-system-ideas.md`). Nothing here is decided — this is a holding space so ideas have somewhere to land while the current products keep getting built.

---

## 2026-09-01

### The idea
Pivot (or expand) from one-time static products (PDFs) toward an all-encompassing life-improvement course or subscription — daily streaming, helping people "get their mind right." Not primarily profit-driven; partly a response to a sense that the internet/social media landscape is shifting fast enough that a month spent heads-down on static products carries real risk of missing where attention actually is.

### Status
Not decided. Sitting with it while continuing to build the current products (Time Management System, Journal) with this in mind. Current plan: don't treat it as either/or — the block/journal content already being built is natural material for a course or daily stream regardless of which direction wins out. Keep building products; let this file catch anything relevant that comes up along the way.

### Signal from product work: dislike of the paper mechanism
While reviewing the printed Time Management System, user said outright they don't like "the paper blocks" — but acknowledged we have to start there for now. Relevant to this pivot file because it's the same underlying tension: a physical/static medium (paper worksheets) vs. something more dynamic (app, digital board, live format). Not acted on yet — still building the paper version as planned — but worth remembering this is a recurring itch, not a one-off complaint.

### Open questions (not yet explored)
- What would the daily stream/subscription actually consist of — teaching the block system live? Live check-ins? Community accountability?
- Relationship to the existing products: are they the free/entry funnel into the subscription, standalone, or fully absorbed into it eventually?
- Platform/format for streaming.
- Pricing/business model for a subscription vs. one-time product sales.
