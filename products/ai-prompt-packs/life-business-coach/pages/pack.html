How To Use This Pack

Get More From Every Prompt

Four categories. 10 prompts each. Copy, customize, paste, go.

1

Pick a prompt that matches what you're working on this week — a session, a post, a follow-up email, a new offer.

2

Replace anything in [brackets] with your specifics — your niche, your client's situation, your price point. The more specific you are, the better the output.

3

Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or whatever AI tool you use. No special access or plugins needed.

4

Treat the output as a first draft, not a final answer. You know your clients and your voice — edit until it sounds like you.

5

Keep a running note of which prompts actually save you time. Those are the ones worth turning into your own templates.

A note on client information: Never paste identifying client details into a public AI tool. Anonymize names, companies, and specifics before you paste anything in.
