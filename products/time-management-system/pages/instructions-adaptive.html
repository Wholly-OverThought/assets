Time Management System · Adaptive Edition · Draft

How This System Works

For chronic pain, limited mobility, or a body that doesn't run on a fixed schedule.

This system is built out of Blocks — small groups of tasks (Hygiene, Body, Doctors & Diagnoses, Relationships, and whatever else you add). You arrange Blocks into your day in the order that makes sense. When the day goes sideways — and with a variable-capacity body, it often will — you move Blocks around instead of abandoning them. A moved Block is not a missed Block.

Neuroscience, psychology, and older sources of wisdom — the Bible among them — agree on more than you'd expect about how the mind works, which is not quite the same thing as the brain. One point they converge on: grouping related things together makes each one easier to remember and easier to actually do. That's the whole mechanism behind a Block. But the point of this system isn't the Blocks themselves — it's using them to find your optimal routine, on whatever kind of day you're having. Flexibility isn't a workaround here. It's the entire point.

The Core Idea, Adapted Three of the Blocks in this edition (Hygiene, Body, Doctors & Diagnoses) are tiered by capacity, not fixed by calendar. Each has a Tier 1 (flare/low-capacity), Tier 2 (moderate), and Tier 3 (higher-capacity) version. Pick whichever tier matches your body today. Completing any tier finishes the Block — there is no lesser version of done.

1Set Your Blocks

Open the Blocks page. Hygiene, Body, and Doctors & Diagnoses each have a full tiered version on their own page. Use the ones provided, edit them, or build your own.

2Lay Out Your Day

Each morning, use the Daily Organizer page to write your Blocks into the order you intend to run them — and note which tier you're aiming for on the tiered Blocks. It's a plan, not a contract.

3Work the Blocks, Check In

Hourly — Prayer / Meditation

A 1–5 minute pause, roughly once an hour. Check or color in the next circle in the row on the Daily Organizer — one per hour, in order.

Every 15–30 Min — Mindfulness Alarm

A short reset: where am I, what am I doing, what does my body need right now? Check or color in the next box in the grid, in sequence.

4When Your Capacity Changes

Drop a tier, don't drop the Block. Moving from a Tier 3 day to a Tier 1 day mid-day is not falling behind — it's the system working as intended. The goal is to finish your Blocks, at whatever size the day allows.

Most Important If you miss something, write it on the Skip Block so it doesn't just vanish — then let it go. This is a structured guide, not a rulebook. You will miss things. That's expected, not a failure — especially here.

5Morning & Evening

Short check-ins bookend the day on the Check-In page. (A full guided journal is a separate companion product — this is just the quick version that keeps the day anchored.)
