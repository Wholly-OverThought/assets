Doctors & Diagnoses

Replaces the Work/School block. Managing a chronic condition is a job — this is that job's block.

Same tiered logic as the other Adaptive blocks. Some days this block is one line. Some days it's a full slate of calls and forms. Both are the block, done.

Tier 1 Flare / low-capacity day
  • Take medication as scheduled
  • Log today's symptoms / pain level
Tier 2 Moderate / manageable day
  • One small admin task — message a provider, refill a prescription
  • Note a question for your next appointment
Tier 3 Higher-capacity day
  • Schedule or attend an appointment
  • Insurance, billing, or paperwork task
  • Organize records / prep for next visit
A Note
  • Logging a symptom is real work. It counts even when nothing "gets done."
  • Save Tier 3 tasks for days that can actually hold them — a missed call can wait for one.

“Managing the illness is the job today. Clock in anyway.”
