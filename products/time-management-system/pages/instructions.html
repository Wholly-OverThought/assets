How This System Works

Read this once. Then keep it nearby for the first week.

This system is built out of Blocks — small groups of tasks (Hygiene, Body, Power Hour, Home, Finance, Relationships, and whatever else you add). You arrange Blocks into your day in the order that makes sense. When the day goes sideways — and it will — you move Blocks around instead of abandoning them. A moved Block is not a missed Block.

Neuroscience, psychology, and older sources of wisdom — the Bible among them — agree on more than you'd expect about how the mind works, which is not quite the same thing as the brain. One point they converge on: grouping related things together makes each one easier to remember and easier to actually do. That's the whole mechanism behind a Block — similar tasks kept together so your mind isn't context-switching all day. But the point of this system isn't the Blocks themselves. It's using them, day after day, to find your optimal routine — and flexibility is what makes that routine something you can actually keep doing.

The Core Idea Success is built by intentionally breaking things into micro steps. Small, specific tasks (“brush teeth,” not “hygiene”) are easier to start, easier to finish, and easier to feel good about finishing. Stack enough small wins and the day takes care of itself.

1Set Your Blocks

Open the Blocks page. Each Block is a labeled group of small tasks. Use the ones provided, edit them, or build your own — the tasks inside the box matter more than the label on it.

2Lay Out Your Day

Each morning, use the Daily Organizer page to write your Blocks into the order you intend to run them. This is a plan, not a contract — it's written in pencil for a reason.

3Work the Blocks, Check In

Hourly — Prayer / Meditation

A 1–5 minute pause, roughly once an hour. Check or color in the next circle in the row on the Daily Organizer — one per hour, in order, left to right.

Every 15–30 Min — Mindfulness Alarm

A short reset: where am I, what am I doing, is it what I planned? Check or color in the next box in the grid — in sequence, not whichever one you like.

4When Something Interrupts You

Don't quit the day — reorganize it. Cross the Block out of its old spot on the Daily Organizer and write it into a new one. The goal is to finish your Blocks, not to finish them on schedule.

Most Important If you miss something, write it on the Skip Block so it doesn't just vanish — then let it go. This is a structured guide, not a rulebook. You will miss things. That's expected, not a failure.

5Morning & Evening

Short check-ins bookend the day on the Check-In page. (A full guided journal is a separate companion product — this is just the quick version that keeps the day anchored.)
