Hygiene — Adaptive

Basic care counts as care, no matter how small the version is today.

This is a drop-in replacement for the standard Hygiene block. On a bad day, "brush teeth" can be the hardest task on the whole page. Pick the tier that matches your body today. Any tier, completed, is the block done — there's no smaller or lesser version of finishing.

Tier 1 Flare / low-capacity day
  • Brush teeth (in bed is fine)
  • Wipe face/body with a washcloth or wipes
  • Deodorant
  • Fresh shirt, if you have the energy
Tier 2 Moderate / manageable day
  • Sit-down shower or sponge bath
  • Brush teeth at the sink
  • Comb or brush hair
Tier 3 Higher-capacity day
  • Full shower
  • Shave, if that's part of your routine
  • Full routine, whatever that looks like for you
A Note
  • Tier 1 is not "not doing hygiene." It's doing hygiene, at the size today allows.
  • If a task isn't safe or possible right now, skip it — the block is still complete.
