Body — Adaptive

For chronic pain, limited mobility, or days your body doesn't cooperate. Rest is a completed task.

This is a drop-in replacement for the standard Body block. Instead of one fixed list, pick the tier that matches your body today — not the tier you think you should be able to do. Checking off a Tier 1 day is just as complete as checking off a Tier 3 day. There is no wrong tier.

Tier 1 Flare / low-capacity day
  • 5 slow breaths, hand on belly
  • Change position or adjust support
  • Ankle pumps or wrist circles (seated/lying down)
  • Note today's pain/energy level, 0–10
Tier 2 Moderate / manageable day
  • Gentle stretch, pain-free range only
  • Short walk — to another room, at your pace
  • Range-of-motion set: neck, shoulders, or hips
Tier 3 Higher-capacity day
  • Longer walk or gentle stretch session
  • Prescribed PT / physical therapy exercises
  • Anything from Tier 1 or 2, if you want more
Pacing, Not Pushing
  • Stop at comfortable effort — not at pain onset.
  • A big day today can mean a harder day tomorrow. When unsure, take the lower tier.
  • Choosing Tier 1 is not falling behind. It's the block, completed.
